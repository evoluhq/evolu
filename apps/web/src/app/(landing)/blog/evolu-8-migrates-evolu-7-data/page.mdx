When we released [Evolu 8](/blog/evolu-8-is-released), we asked apps with Evolu 7 user data to stay on Evolu 7, because Evolu 8 couldn't migrate it. We haven't forgotten those apps. Evolu 8 migrates their data now.

The first time an app updated from Evolu 7 starts, Evolu moves the Evolu 7 database on the device into Evolu 8 and then deletes it, on the web and on React Native. The owner comes across with its mnemonic, and so does the data: what is synced, what never reached a relay, and local-only tables. Users do nothing.

## What the app does

- An app that let Evolu 7 create its owner uses `createEvoluApp` with instant start and `createFirstAppOwner: true`. Its first AppOwner is the Evolu 7 owner.
- An app that gave Evolu 7 its own owner passes that owner to `createEvolu`.
- Evolu 8 renamed Evolu 7's `name` option to `appName`. An app whose Evolu 7 `name` differs from its `appName` passes it as `evolu7Name`.

Before you release the update, read [Upgrading from Evolu 7](/docs/upgrading#evolu-7-to-evolu-8). Two things matter most: never roll a migrated device back to an Evolu 7 build, and while a tab still runs Evolu 7, the updated app doesn't start until that tab closes.

## How we tested it

The tests don't fake Evolu 7. They create real databases with the published Evolu 7.4.1 and let Evolu 8 migrate them: in Node.js, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, and with React Native's SQLite driver. They also sync an Evolu 7 device and an Evolu 8 device of one owner through a relay, in both directions, because users don't update all their devices at once.

If your app waited on Evolu 7, it can update now. If something doesn't come across as you expect, please [open an issue](https://github.com/evoluhq/evolu/issues).
